## NV sound banks

NV Glass uses modified Kenney Interface Sounds (CC0), mixed with original NV synthesizer tones. NV Tactical uses a trimmed and level-adjusted Kenney Impact Sounds sample (CC0).
Sources: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds and https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds. License texts are in `licenses/kenney-interface-sounds.txt` and `licenses/kenney-impact-sounds.txt`.
